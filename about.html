About

Change Here For… is a small, independent quiz about London's rail network.

Not an official game

This site is not affiliated with, endorsed by or connected to Transport for London. Line names and colours are used only to say which line is which. For travel information, use TfL's own website and apps.

Where the data comes from

The list of stations and the lines that serve them was compiled from public sources, including Wikipedia's lists of London Underground and DLR stations and the published network map (September 2026). It covers zones 1 and 2, including stations on the zone 2/3 boundary.

The network changes, and mistakes happen. If you spot one, email hello@changeherefor.com with the station and what's wrong.

Your data

There are no accounts. Your progress stays on your device. See the privacy notice.
