Privacy

Last updated 7 November 2026.

The short version

There are no accounts, no cookies, no ads and no tracking. Your game progress is stored in your own browser and never sent to us.

Your saved progress

To save your progress, the game uses your browser's local storage. It holds your current run, your best scores and which stations you've answered. It stays on your device; we can't see it. You can delete it at any time with "Delete my progress" on the home page, or by clearing this site's data in your browser.

Visitor statistics

We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to count visits and see which pages are used. It doesn't use cookies, doesn't track you across sites and doesn't identify you.

Hosting

The site is hosted by Cloudflare. Like any web host, Cloudflare processes technical information such as IP addresses to deliver the site and protect it from attacks.
